Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This first version of the Hair by Sara website runs in your browser. It does not send booking details to a salon server. When you submit the form, the booking is saved only in the local browser storage on this device and a confirmation appears on screen. It does not automatically notify the salon.

Information entered

The booking form asks for your name, phone number, appointment date and time, and optionally your email address and chosen style. These details are stored in this browser so the local owner dashboard can display them. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to view or remove them.

Photos and browser storage

Photos added through the owner dashboard are kept in this browser's local storage. They are not uploaded to a server. Clearing browser data may remove saved styles, photos, hours, and bookings.

Contact

For questions about a booking or your information, call 0413 325 647.

This policy describes the current local website prototype. It should be reviewed and updated before the site is hosted or starts collecting information through an online service.
