Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

When online booking is available, the information submitted through the booking form is sent to Hair by Sara’s Supabase database so the salon can manage the request. If the shared booking service is unavailable, the site will not submit a booking and will direct clients to call instead.

Information collected

A booking includes your name, phone number, selected hairstyle, price, appointment date and time, and optionally your email address. The salon owner can view and manage these details in the private dashboard. A request is not confirmed until the salon updates its status or contacts you.

Photos and saved data

Hairstyle photographs are stored in Supabase Storage and displayed publicly in the style catalogue. Crop and framing settings are stored separately from each original photo. Older salon data may remain in browser storage on the device where it was created until it is backed up and migrated. Supabase Auth stores the owner’s signed-in session in the browser.

Payments and customer accounts

The website does not collect payment information and does not provide customer accounts.

Contact

For questions about a booking or your information, call 0413 325 647.
