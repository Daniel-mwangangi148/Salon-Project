Appointment requests

Submitting a request sends it to the salon’s shared booking system when online booking is available. A request remains pending until the owner confirms it. If the booking system is unavailable, the form will not accept the request; call 0413 325 647 instead.

Prices and services

Prices are displayed in Australian dollars. The selected style and price are recorded with each request. Contact Hair by Sara if you have questions about a style or its price.

Availability

Available times follow the salon’s configured working hours in Canberra time. The shared system checks availability when a request is submitted and rejects a time that has already been taken. Appointment duration is not yet included in slot calculations.

Contact

Call 0413 325 647, Monday to Sunday, 10:00 am to 7:00 pm.
